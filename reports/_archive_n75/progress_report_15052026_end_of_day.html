2. Cell K — Exomiser HPO-only baseline

The single most important addition today. Without this number, "Cell D = 0.627" was uninterpretable. Now we know geno_agent sits ~15 pp below the established curated-database baseline.

Why HPO-only (not full variant prioritisation)

Our pipeline takes HPO terms + a candidate gene list. It does not take variants. Comparing against Exomiser-full would be comparing an HPO-only system against an HPO + variant system — Exomiser-full would win by construction (more information). The thesis claim is narrower: "for phenotype-driven gene-prioritisation, literature-RAG matches curated-database approaches". Cell K HPO-only is the right anchor.

3. Cell P — Why naive D + K rank fusion fails

Hypothesis: combine D + K since they have complementary category strengths. Use RRF (Reciprocal Rank Fusion), the standard score-free IR ensemble.

Result on n=75: Cell P top-1 = 0.653 — slightly above D (0.627) but well below K (0.773). No choice of (w_D, w_K) in the weight sweep lifts RRF past K's 0.773 on top-1.

Reading the signal

Zero regressions. No case where D got rank-1 was demoted by the rerank. The Critic on reranked top-10 chunks correctly identifies the same causal evidence as Cell D did with the original top-10.

Catastrophic recoveries. ADRA2A (50→1) and ARPC5 (50→1) — cases Cell D got completely wrong are now correct. The cross-encoder is surfacing causal chunks Cell D's hybrid retrieval was burying.

Mechanism

Cell D's hybrid retrieval (BM25 + dense) ranks chunks by lexical overlap and dense similarity. The cross-encoder scores chunks by attended relevance — it can spot causal evidence in chunks that don't share many surface tokens with the query.

For the cases where Cell D's retrieval is the binding constraint, this is exactly the fix. The Critic + Synth on top of reranked chunks then converts them into correct top-1 picks.
