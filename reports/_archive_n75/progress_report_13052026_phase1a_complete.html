Difficulties resolved this window (capsule)

  1. PMC OA S3 bucket flattened — switched to NCBI FTP bulk.
  2. aws s3 sync too slow (~12 days). Tried s5cmd for 5–10× speedup.
  3. 3 BIOS-screen reboots in 24 h forced abandonment of multi-day WSL syncs.
  4. Worker race condition in extraction (40 crashes) — fixed with per-worker tempfile.TemporaryDirectory().
  5. 12 truncated JSONLs survived idempotency check — fixed with atomic .partial → rename.
  6. Chunker OOM at 62 GB — fixed with batched pool.map + /home ext4 output.
  7. Schema mismatch — added 03_normalize_dedupe_filter.py as the bridge step.
  8. Disk pressure on /mnt/c — moved tooling to /home.
