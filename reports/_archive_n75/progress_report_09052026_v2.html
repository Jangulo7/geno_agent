6Production build — kicking off now

After this report is committed, the production PMC OA full-corpus download is being launched in the background:

nohup bash scripts/corpus/01_download_pmc_oa.sh \
      > logs/download_pmc_oa.stdout 2>&1 &
disown
Estimated wall-clock: 5–9 days across all of [5a]–[6]. Dominated by ~24–48 GPU hours of embedding once the pipeline gets there. The download itself is ~3–8 hours at your network bandwidth.

Monitor progress

tail -f logs/download_pmc_oa.log         # live log of S3 sync
du -sh /mnt/c/pmc_workspace/xml_raw/     # disk growth over time
ps -p $(pgrep -f 01_download_pmc_oa)     # process check

Stop early if needed

pkill -f 01_download_pmc_oa.sh           # graceful (S3 sync is resumable on rerun)
