0. Executive Summary

🏆 Headline finding (n=1,047)

Cell S (rerank + LEA) statistically outperforms Exomiser HPO-only on overall top-1 (0.725 vs 0.691, Δ = +3.4 pp, paired-bootstrap 95 % CI [+0.006, +0.064]), plus on metabolic (+8.4 pp) and immunological (+6.7 pp) MONDO subgroups, while remaining statistically equivalent on developmental and neurological subgroups.

LEA contributes a statistically significant +2.7 pp on top of cross-encoder reranking alone. The immunological lead claim survives 100 % leave-one-out sensitivity on n=300 cases (McNemar exact p = 0.0076).

1. Motivation and trigger

1.1 Why a paper extension at all?

The thesis (n=75) reported S=0.787 vs K=0.773, Δ=+1.3 pp — but with CI half-width ≈ ±0.10 the conservative framing was statistical parity. For a Q1 paper the CI needed to drop to ±0.04.

1.2 Why both v1 (n=459) and v2 (n=1047)?

v1 ran in May 2026 with n=459 from Phenopacket Store v0.1.19 (immunological capped at 85 eligible cases). Result: overall exact parity on top-1 (Δ=+0.000); immunological win Δ=+0.118 was marginal — CI lower bound at exactly +0.000.

A sensitivity analysis on the v1 n=85 immunological subset revealed it was fragile: only 67/85 (78.8 %) of leave-one-out subsets preserved CI > 0; 18 cases were load-bearing. A Q1 reviewer would justifiably downgrade to "trends toward outperforming".

1.3 The v0.1.26 discovery (Step 0)

A 30-minute Phenopacket Store version audit revealed v0.1.26 (released 2026-01-13) had 25+ new IEI cohorts added since v0.1.19, growing the eligible immunological pool from 85 → 390 cases (+359 %). Net cohort: 422 → 674 gene cohorts; 6,668 → 9,588 phenopackets.

6.1 v1 Cell S contamination (4 attempts before fix)

The first 4 Cell S attempts failed in different ways, each fixed in turn:

6.2 v2 false threshold abort (the threshold was too strict)
Symptom: v2 GPU sequencer completed Cell D and Cell L cleanly, started vLLM successfully (HTTP 200, app ready), but immediately aborted with exit rc=11 from assert_gpu_free("after-vllm-loaded"). nvidia-smi showed free=5,887 MiB — exactly 113 MiB below the 6,000 MiB safety threshold.

Root cause: the 6,000 MiB threshold was set conservatively for the old util=0.55 config. With current util=0.75, vLLM legitimately consumes ~24.4 GB → 8 GB free expected → momentarily dips to ~5.9 GB during CUDA-graph capture.

Recovery: launched run_paper_extension_S_only.sh with MIN_FREE_MIB=4000 env override. vLLM started cleanly (KV cache 8.25 GiB, max concurrency 1.83×), Cell S ran to completion in 7.7 h with zero HTTP 400s and 2 / 1,047 LEA-JSON-parse fallbacks (0.19 %).
6.3 v2 Stage 17 hardcoded path bug
Symptom: Stage 17 silently ignored TEST_CASES_DIR env var and clobbered the thesis n=75 05_validated.jsonl instead of writing to data/test_cases_1050/.

Fix: changed TC_DIR to honour the env var (identical pattern to Stages 14/15/18/19). Recoverable — the n=75 file is a derived artefact and re-runs deterministically. Committed as fcbd426.
6.4 v2 Stage 16 lacked per-category target support
Symptom: v2 disproportionate sampling required 250+300+250+250 but 16_stratified_sample.py only supported global --target-size with ceil/4 split.

Fix: added --per-category-target "cat=N,cat=N,..." CLI flag. Backwards-compatible.
6.5 v2 RNU4-2 drops at Stage 18 (3 cases / 1,050 = 0.29 %)

3 cases dropped because the causal gene was not in the HGNC protein-coding set: RNU4-2 (×2) is a small nuclear RNA recently linked to a neurodevelopmental disorder via splicing defects. Our candidate-list distractor draw requires protein-coding genes, so non-coding causal genes cannot enter the test set. Known scope limitation.

6.6 v2 LEA-JSON-parse fallbacks (2 / 1,047)

2 cases triggered LEA JSON response invalid; falling back to deterministic synth warnings during Cell S: IRF4:PMID_36662884_P4 and KCNH5:PMID_36307226_Proband_15. In both cases vLLM responded HTTP 200 with content that failed json.loads() (likely truncated or formatted with extra prose). The fallback ranked these 2 cases with the deterministic synthesizer (= Cell L behavior).

Decision: leave as-is, do NOT re-roll.
  • The fallback is a documented architectural safety feature.
  • Re-rolling failures = cherry-picking → upward bias.
  • Numerical impact: at most ±0.19 pp shift — invisible in 3-decimal reporting.
  • 0.19 % fallback rate is a useful robustness measurement to report.

8. Discussion

What this result says. Literature-grounded agentic RAG with cross-encoder reranking and LLM-based evidence aggregation statistically outperforms Exomiser HPO-only on top-1 gene prioritisation at n=1,047. The advantage holds robustly on metabolic and immunological MONDO subgroups and is statistically equivalent on developmental and neurological subgroups. This is, to our knowledge, the first n>1,000 demonstration that an unsupervised literature-only system (no curated phenotype-gene tables, no MIM symptom hierarchies) beats Exomiser HPO-only on top-1.
What this result does NOT say. Exomiser remains superior on top-5, top-10 and NDCG@10 by 2-4 pp. For deployment patterns that need broad recall (e.g., panel diagnostics), Exomiser is still the better tool. This is HPO-only on both sides — no genotype/VCF input. n=1,047 is a single random sample — five-seed stability has not been verified. No direct comparison to DeepRare or LA-MARRVEL yet (pending Strategy A items).

Why the v2 absolute scores dropped vs v1

v1 absolute top-1 was ~0.767 for both S and K. v2 is ~0.69-0.73. The drop is real and informative: v0.1.26 added 252 new gene cohorts (+59 % cohort growth) — many for newly-described diseases with sparser literature. K dropped by 7.6 pp; S dropped by 4.2 pp. S dropped less than K, which is why S now statistically wins where v1 tied.

Per-MONDO complementarity is a genuine finding

CategoryMechanism
immunological (S wins)IEI phenotypes (autoinflammatory cycles, complement deficiencies) don't compress well into curated tables. Cross-encoder + LEA reads prose evidence that the table loses.
metabolic (S wins)Biochemical case reports describe pathway context (Krebs cycle, sulfur metabolism) that disambiguates similar-presentation genes. Prose retrieves what tables compress.
developmental (K leads)Exomiser's database is densely populated for well-characterised dev syndromes (Robinow, Cornelia de Lange, Kabuki). The curated table has structural advantages here.

A clinical deployment could use the two methods together: Exomiser as a recall-first short-lister and geno_agent as a precision-first re-ranker.

11. Conclusions

  1. Cell S statistically outperforms Exomiser HPO-only on overall top-1 at n=1,047 (Δ=+3.4 pp, 95 % CI [+0.006, +0.064]).
  2. The win is robust on metabolic (+8.4 pp) and immunological (+6.7 pp) MONDO subgroups, with the immunological lead claim surviving 100 % leave-one-out sensitivity (McNemar p=0.008).
  3. Categorical complementarity is the main qualitative finding: K and S excel on different disease types.
  4. Cross-encoder reranking is the single largest architectural contributor (+23.8 pp on top-1 vs deterministic multi-agent hybrid); LEA adds a smaller but statistically significant +2.7 pp.
  5. Exomiser retains advantages on top-5/10 and NDCG@10 — reported honestly as a caveat.
  6. The v1 "parity" framing was an underestimate; the v2 result confirms a real, significant overall S advantage.
  7. Operationally: 4-cell × 1,047-case evaluation completed in ~20 h wall on a single RTX 5090, with zero GPU crashes after initial VRAM-cap calibration. Reproducible with one launcher invocation.
